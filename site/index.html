THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Learning from
what learning leaves behind.

A trained network holds a record of the task it learned. Can another network make sense of that record?

We study this through controlled arithmetic networks and image classifiers. Lens readers use weights, structured neuron records and, in some experiments, activations to predict target behavior or reconstruct missing parameters.
