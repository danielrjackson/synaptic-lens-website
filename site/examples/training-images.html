IMAGE-RECOGNITION TARGETS

See what the classifiers learn from.

Real training images from our custom datasets: sprites, shapes, textures, counting, spatial location and arrow direction.

Each card shows three examples of the same class. These are the original 28 × 28 grayscale pixels, enlarged to make them easy to inspect. Click an image to open its PNG file.

These images train the target classifiers. Lens studies the classifiers' networks to learn about their capabilities.

About these samples

The gallery contains 180 examples from the redesigned custom-image TRAIN collection used in SYN94: the first three rows of each class in one training root per family. It shows the data used for those classifiers; the released MNIST Lens example is a separate study of handwritten-digit classifiers.

The redesigned images, including the repaired sprite set, were generated by this project's custom dataset code. Their pixels and labels were checked against the archived data manifest. Sample rows, labels and checksums · Target model factory ↗
